Inclusive by direction

Building an accessible job-search experience.

Job Seeker Copilot is being designed with accessibility as a long-term priority. As the platform develops, we plan to improve compatibility with assistive technologies, strengthen keyboard navigation and work towards recognised accessibility best practices.

Accessibility work will continue throughout development and after the beta release. This is a statement of direction, not a claim of certification or current WCAG compliance.

Read the Accessibility Statement

Development priorities

  • Clear, consistent navigation and plain English
  • Readable typography and strong colour contrast
  • Responsive layouts and accessible forms
  • Keyboard and screen-reader compatibility
  • Reduced-motion support where movement is used
  • Testing with assistive technology and real users
  • Working towards WCAG 2.2 Level AA
  • Continuous review as functionality changes
