How Job Seeker Copilot Fits In

Job boards help you find opportunities. Job Seeker Copilot is being built to connect the complete process.

LinkedIn, Indeed and other job platforms already provide valuable features such as vacancy search, saved jobs, application tracking and CV support. Specialist platforms also offer combinations of job matching, document creation and application-management tools.

Job Seeker Copilot is not based on the claim that these tools do not exist. It is being developed as a UK-focused workspace that connects each vacancy with its match information, tailored documents, application status and activity history.

The comparison shows that many platforms already solve important parts of the job-search process. Job Seeker Copilot’s role is not to reproduce every feature in isolation. Its purpose is to create a connected workflow in which a vacancy, its match information, its tailored documents and its application history remain together.

The initial focus is UK jobseekers who want greater structure and visibility across their search while remaining responsible for reviewing and submitting every application.
