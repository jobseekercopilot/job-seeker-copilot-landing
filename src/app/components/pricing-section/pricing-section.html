What uses a document generation?

One successfully delivered tailored CV or one successfully delivered cover letter uses one generation. A complete pair uses two. A regeneration is a new document and uses another generation after you choose it. Failed or cancelled delivery uses none; a fallback that is successfully delivered uses one.

What does not use a generation?

Failed or cancelled generation, duplicate or replayed requests, fallback attempts that do not deliver a usable document, uploads, editing, downloads and exports. Any unfinished reservation is released.

Each displayed price is the total charged at checkout for customers with a UK billing address. Tax details, where applicable, are confirmed before payment.
