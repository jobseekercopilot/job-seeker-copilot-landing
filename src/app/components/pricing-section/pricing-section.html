What uses a document credit?

One successfully delivered tailored CV or one successfully delivered cover letter uses one credit. A complete pair uses two. A regeneration is a new document and uses another credit after you choose it.

What does not use a credit?

Failed or cancelled generation, duplicate or replayed requests, identified no-charge fallbacks, uploads, editing, downloads and exports. Any unfinished reservation is released.

Prices are fixed GBP totals for customers with a UK billing address. Tax treatment, where applicable, is confirmed before payment.
