UK labour market

The challenge facing UK job seekers.

Finding work can involve searching across several websites, preparing different application documents and tracking multiple opportunities. Job Seeker Copilot is designed to bring those activities together in one organised workspace.

These figures provide national context only. They do not measure Job Seeker Copilot’s effectiveness, imply government endorsement or predict an individual job-search outcome.
