Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Job Seeker Copilot handles personal information collected through this pre-launch website.

Effective {{ effectiveDate }}.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies when you visit the Job Seeker Copilot landing page, join the early-access list or send us an enquiry. The main Job Seeker Copilot product is still in development; a separate product privacy notice will be provided before public accounts are opened.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed for the service you choose to use:

  • Waitlist details: the email address you submit, confirmation and unsubscribe status, consent version and trusted timestamps for product availability and early-access updates.
  • Contact details: your name, email address, message subject and message when you use the contact form.
  • Technical information: limited server, security and diagnostic information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps may be processed by our hosting provider.

We do not ask for a password, payment information, CV, employment history or application documents on this website.

3. How we use information

We use waitlist email addresses to send a confirmation email and, only after confirmation, important Job Seeker Copilot availability and early-access updates. Each update will support unsubscribe. We use contact-form information only to understand and respond to the enquiry; sending a contact message does not add anyone to the waiting list. Technical information may be used to operate, secure and improve the website.

The early-access offer does not add any fields to the waiting-list form. We use the same submitted email address and confirmation status to manage waitlist communications and potential early-access eligibility; we do not ask for employment details, phone numbers, CVs, referrals or profiling information for this offer.

Where UK data-protection law applies, the intended lawful bases are consent for optional waitlist communications and our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, protecting the service and understanding basic website performance. You may withdraw consent to waitlist updates at any time.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This version of the website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. Essential infrastructure may use short-lived technical storage or process request information to deliver and secure the site. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy and any consent controls will be updated before they are enabled.

5. Storage, sharing and international transfers

Waitlist addresses and consent records are intended to be stored in Amazon DynamoDB and processed by Amazon SES for email delivery when those services are configured. Contact messages are intended to be delivered through Amazon SES to an authorised private mailbox; optional short-term database storage is disabled by default and will be disclosed here if enabled. Access is limited to authorised operators and contracted providers that host, deliver or secure the service. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

If a provider processes information outside the UK, we will use an approved transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required.

6. How long we keep information

Unconfirmed waitlist records are designed to expire after a configurable short period (initial infrastructure default: seven days). Confirmed and unsubscribed records will be kept only while the early-access list and suppression record remain necessary, or until an approved deletion request, under a final retention schedule that must be set before launch. Contact messages will normally exist in the receiving mailbox and be retained only as long as needed to respond, keep an appropriate business record and meet legal obligations. If optional database storage is enabled, it will use a configurable expiry. Security logs are retained for a limited operational period.

7. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, HTTPS in production and restricted use of service credentials. No internet service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on your circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your personal information, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Children

This website is intended for people managing their own job search and is not directed at children under 16. Please do not submit a child’s personal information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Job Seeker Copilot develops or its providers change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Privacy questions, stored-data export requests and deletion requests can be sent to {{ contact.privacy }}. Waiting-list messages will include a link that immediately marks the address unsubscribed without requiring an account.

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Job Seeker Copilot is being developed with the aim of making job-search planning and application support usable by as many people as possible.

Last reviewed {{ effectiveDate }}. Accessibility is an active area of development, not a certification claim.

Our current position

Job Seeker Copilot and this public website are still under active development. Accessibility is an ongoing priority, and improvements will continue before and after the beta release. We do not currently claim conformance with WCAG, formal certification or independent accessibility approval.

What this website currently supports

The landing page uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible form labels, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus indicators, responsive layouts, status announcements for forms and reduced-motion preferences. These measures improve access but do not establish full accessibility or product-wide compliance.

Development goals

Future work will focus on clear and consistent navigation, readable typography, colour contrast, accessible forms, keyboard-only use, screen-reader compatibility, reduced-motion support and plain English. The longer-term target is to work towards WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Testing and continuous improvement

Planned testing includes automated accessibility checks, keyboard-only journeys, screen-reader testing, colour-contrast review and usability feedback from people with different access needs. New product functionality will need continuing review rather than a one-off assessment.

Report an accessibility problem

If something makes this website or product difficult to use, email {{ contact.generalEnquiries }} with the page, task, device and assistive technology involved. Feedback will help prioritise and verify future improvements. Please do not include sensitive job-search information unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Statement review

This statement will be reviewed as the service changes, after meaningful accessibility testing and before public launch. Any future conformance claim will be based on evidence available at that time.
