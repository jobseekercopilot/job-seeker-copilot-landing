1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies when you visit the website, join the updates list, send an enquiry or use a public-beta account. The in-product notice provides additional detail at the point of use.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed for the service you choose to use:

  • Waitlist details: the email address you submit, pending or confirmed status, consent version, source, timestamps and hashed single-use confirmation-token information.
  • Email-delivery details: Amazon SES delivery, hard-bounce and complaint events used to protect recipients and suppress further messages where necessary.
  • Contact details: your name, email address, message subject and message when you use the contact form.
  • Optional aggregate analytics: only after you choose “Allow analytics”, we may record a controlled event name, approved page path, form location, broad viewport category, production or smoke-test traffic class, a coarse acquisition category and approved non-personal campaign labels.
  • Technical information: limited server, security and diagnostic information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps may be processed by our hosting provider.
  • Product account and profile: identity, sign-in security, job preferences, career evidence, saved vacancies, applications and support records.
  • Documents and AI requests: uploaded and generated CVs or cover letters, the selected vacancy and relevant evidence sent to contracted AI services to provide the requested generation.
  • Payments: order, entitlement, refund and dispute records. Stripe processes card details; Job Seeker Copilot does not store full card numbers.

The public website does not ask for a product password, card details, CV, employment history or application documents.

3. How we use information

When you submit the waiting-list form, the address is stored as pending and Amazon SES sends a confirmation email. The address becomes confirmed only after its secure, single-use link is used. Launch and early-access messages must not be sent to pending records. Each future update will support unsubscribe.

Joining the updates list does not reserve a product account or determine eligibility for a purchase promotion. Any live offer is controlled by the product’s payment service and is confirmed only if it is reserved for a specific checkout.

We use contact-form information only to understand and respond to the enquiry; sending a contact message does not add anyone to the waiting list. Technical information may be used to operate, secure and improve the website. Optional aggregate analytics help us understand which public pages and forms are useful and whether the waiting-list and contact journeys work.

Where UK data-protection law applies, the intended lawful bases are consent for optional waitlist communications and our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, protecting the service and understanding basic website performance. You may withdraw consent to waitlist updates at any time.

For product accounts, processing is necessary to perform the service contract; security, fraud prevention and service improvement rely on legitimate interests; legal and financial records are retained where required by law. AI assists document preparation but Job Seeker Copilot does not make hiring decisions or automatically submit applications.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising cookies, advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, fingerprinting or visitor profiles. Optional first-party analytics are disabled by default and send nothing until you choose “Allow analytics”. “Refuse analytics” is presented at the same level, the website continues to work after refusal and you can revisit the choice through “Analytics choices” in the footer.

Your choice is stored in local browser storage against this policy version. After acceptance, session storage may hold only controlled campaign labels, a coarse acquisition category, a production-or-smoke marker and a boolean that prevents the same browser tab from counting more than one visit. These values contain no visitor identifier and expire with the browser session, except for the saved choice.

The analytics request does not include a name, email address, contact message, confirmation token, full URL, raw referrer, IP address, user-agent value or advertising identifier. Campaign values are limited to an approved non-personal vocabulary. The dashboard reports visits as a privacy-preserving session count, not as a count of unique people.

5. Storage, sharing and international transfers

Waitlist addresses and registration records are stored in Amazon DynamoDB. Amazon SES processes the address to deliver confirmation and later permitted messages. Hard-bounce and complaint information may be retained for suppression and compliance so further email is not sent improperly. Contact messages are intended to be delivered through Amazon SES to an authorised private mailbox. Optional analytics events are sent to our own restricted AWS collector and CloudWatch logs in the deployed UK Region (currently eu-west-2); AWS necessarily processes network traffic to provide that infrastructure, while our analytics event and API access-log design omits full IP addresses and user-agent values. Access is limited to authorised operators and contracted providers that host, deliver or secure the service. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

If a provider processes information outside the UK, we use an applicable UK adequacy regulation, UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum and appropriate supplementary safeguards where required.

6. How long we keep information

Confirmation links expire after 48 hours by default. Pending records are configured for deletion after 30 days if confirmation is not completed. Confirmed records remain until unsubscribe, an approved deletion request or the end of the waiting-list purpose. Bounce and complaint suppression records are retained only as long as necessary for compliance and safe delivery. DynamoDB expiry is asynchronous, so deletion may occur shortly after the stated retention point rather than immediately.

Contact messages will normally exist in the receiving mailbox and be retained only as long as needed to respond, keep an appropriate business record and meet legal obligations. Optional analytics and security log groups have a 30-day retention setting. Aggregate CloudWatch metrics may remain available for operational reporting without containing visitor identifiers or submitted form content.

7. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, HTTPS in production and restricted use of service credentials. No internet service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on your circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or transfer of your personal information, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Age and UK availability

The public beta is intended only for UK residents aged 18 or over who are managing their own job search.

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Job Seeker Copilot is being developed with the aim of making job-search planning and application support usable by as many people as possible.

{{ legalReady ? 'Last reviewed ' + config.legalEffectiveDate + '.' : 'Draft statement — review date not yet supplied.' }} Accessibility is an active area of development, not a certification claim.

Our current position

Job Seeker Copilot and this public website are still under active development. Accessibility is an ongoing priority, and improvements will continue before and after the beta release. We do not currently claim conformance with WCAG, formal certification or independent accessibility approval.

What this website currently supports

The landing page uses semantic headings and landmarks, visible form labels, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus indicators, responsive layouts, status announcements for forms and reduced-motion preferences. These measures improve access but do not establish full accessibility or product-wide compliance.

Development goals

Future work will focus on clear and consistent navigation, readable typography, colour contrast, accessible forms, keyboard-only use, screen-reader compatibility, reduced-motion support and plain English. The longer-term target is to work towards WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Testing and continuous improvement

Planned testing includes automated accessibility checks, keyboard-only journeys, screen-reader testing, colour-contrast review and usability feedback from people with different access needs. New product functionality will need continuing review rather than a one-off assessment.

Report an accessibility problem

If something makes this website or product difficult to use, email {{ contact.generalEnquiries }} with the page, task, device and assistive technology involved. Feedback will help prioritise and verify future improvements. Please do not include sensitive job-search information unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Statement review

This statement will be reviewed as the service changes, after meaningful accessibility testing and before public launch. Any future conformance claim will be based on evidence available at that time.
